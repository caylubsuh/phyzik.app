# Marketing — Ambassador Program

**Owner:** Caleb (program lead), Sophia (athlete recruitment).
**Budget estimate:** $0–2k first 90 days (merch + occasional cash gifts); scales based on tier.
**Grounded in:** `research/competitors.md` §Strong §Hevy §RP, `research/growth-case-studies.md` §Strava §Whoop §Zwift, `lib/team.ts` (existing ambassador / athlete structure).

This is different from the **influencer program** (`influencer-tiers.md`). Ambassadors are long-term, unpaid-or-low-paid partners who are genuine users of PHYZIK and believe in the product. Influencers are transactional campaign partners with deliverables and compensation. The overlap: Tier A ambassadors can become influencers; Tier A influencers can graduate into ambassadors. Different tracks, different goals.

---

## 1. The strategic frame

### 1.1 What an ambassador is (and isn't)

**Ambassador =**
- Uses PHYZIK as their primary training log for ≥8 weeks.
- Creates content (IG posts, Stories, Reels, occasional long-form) that features PHYZIK naturally.
- Wears/carries PHYZIK merch in training content.
- Helps recruit other athletes into the program.
- Gets early access to new features + a voice in programming decisions.

**Ambassador is not:**
- An influencer with a campaign contract. (No minimum post count, no specific hashtags mandated.)
- A staff member. (No salary. No daily obligations.)
- A "gift it and pray" marketing gimmick. (We pick people carefully; we stay in relationship.)

### 1.2 Why ambassadors before paid influencer spend

Every case study in `growth-case-studies.md` that grew before $1M in ad spend used some version of this model: WHOOP's "members first" program, Strava's pro-athlete seeding, Zwift's pro-cyclist partnerships. The math: 5–15 genuine advocates with <50k followings and real social proof beats 1 mega-influencer with 1M followers and an obvious paid caption. Trust compounds.

More practically: we're a pre-launch app. No paid influencer wants to endorse a product they can't evaluate credibly. Ambassadors evaluate the product, live with it, and then endorse it. Different trust signal.

## 2. The tiers

### Tier A — Founding Athletes (4–6 people in first 90 days)

**Profile:**
- 10k+ Instagram following OR verified competitive track record (pro, elite amateur, competed nationally).
- Lifting-adjacent discipline: bodybuilding, powerlifting, Hyrox, strongwoman/strongman, hybrid athletics.
- Existing content practice — posts training content naturally, not just aesthetics.
- Alignment with PHYZIK's voice: evidence-backed, respectful, real. No influencers whose brand is shouty or gimmicky.

- Ethan — (role TBD, already listed on team).

**Goal:** Add 2–3 more Tier A before June. Target disciplines not yet covered: powerlifting (any gender, USA/UK), one strongman/strongwoman, one recovery/mobility specialist.

**What Tier A gets:**
- Featured on `/community` page with photo, bio, discipline.
- Custom referral link (`phyzik.app/i/{name}`) with a personalized landing page.
- Direct line to Caleb + Vijay.
- Early access to every feature. First to try the program builder, first to test Pro, first to break stuff.
- Free PHYZIK merch at release cadence (hoodie, shirts — 2–3 pieces a year).
- Optional: cash honorarium per quarter ($500–1500) for Tier A who are spending significant time creating PHYZIK-specific content. Not a salary, not a contract — a thank-you retainer that scales with the relationship.
- Co-developed programming credit: "Hyrox 16-week block, co-designed with Ten." Permanent on the program.

**What Tier A gives:**
- Logs every training session on PHYZIK for 90 days minimum.
- ~1 piece of content per month naturally featuring the app. No mandated scripts, no required hashtags.
- Refers 3–5 other aligned athletes in their first 6 months.
- Quick responsiveness when we ask for feedback on new features (1-week turnaround).
- Warm intro availability: if Caleb asks "can you introduce me to X athlete," they help if they can.

**The relationship shape:** low-obligation, high-trust. Nobody signs a contract. It's a gentleman's agreement with aligned incentives.

### Tier B — Athlete Partners (15–30 people over 6 months)

**Profile:**
- 1k–10k following OR competitive amateur with a local scene presence.
- Active in a niche: college strength coaches, local competitive powerlifters, CrossFit affiliate coaches, Hyrox regional competitors, specific-city gym owners.
- Authentic voice. Not "fitness model" aesthetic unless that's their actual discipline.
- Willingness to log 8+ weeks on PHYZIK and share publicly.

**What Tier B gets:**
- Featured on `/community` athlete roster (second section, below Founding Athletes).

- Free merch (1–2 pieces).
- Access to a private Discord or Slack channel with Caleb, Sophia, and other Tier A+B.
- Merch at 5 referrals instead of 10 (per `referral-program.md`).
- Early access to features 1–2 weeks ahead of public.

**What Tier B gives:**
- Logs on PHYZIK for 8+ weeks.
- Authentic mention of the app when relevant to their content.
- Refers 1–2 additional aligned people if natural.
- Honest feedback when asked.

### Tier C — Community Ambassadors (unlimited)

**Profile:**
- Anyone who hits 25 referrals (per `referral-program.md` tier).
- Or: gym owners, coaches, college team captains who apply and are approved.
- Smaller following / local reach, but high influence in their specific community.

**What Tier C gets:**
- Named recognition on the /founders page (when we ship it).
- Permanent Founding Member badge.
- Lifetime Pro (when Pro exists) at 50 referrals.
- Access to the broader community Discord.
- First-look at open roles, paid campaigns, etc.

**What Tier C gives:**
- Natural, unprompted usage + sharing.
- Word-of-mouth referrals.

## 3. Recruitment playbook

### 3.1 Where to find Tier A

Sophia owns this, with Caleb support. Targeting:

**Bodybuilding / hypertrophy:**
- Midsize IG accounts (10k–50k) in the RP / Jeff Nippard / Menno Henselmans orbit. Not the principals themselves — people one layer out who are serious lifters with growing audiences.
- Competitive natural bodybuilders (OCB, NANBF, WNBF) whose IG shows real prep.
- NPC Men's Physique competitors with coaching pedigree.
- Avoid: "aesthetic only" accounts, bro-science signatures, enhancement-glorifying content.

**Hyrox / hybrid:**
- Top-50 Hyrox competitors globally (Hunter McIntyre tier out of reach; his training partners and people he trains against — reachable).
- Hyrox coaches at affiliate gyms in major cities (NYC, LA, London, Stockholm, Sydney).
- CrossFit regional-level athletes who've moved into Hyrox.

**Powerlifting:**
- USAPL / IPF raw lifters at 80–100% of elite totals who have a content habit.
- Coaches (not the lifters themselves in many cases) — Stephanie Dorworth-tier coaches with audiences.
- Strongman / strongwoman: mid-tier pros (one rung below Oleksii Novikov / Inez Carrasquillo level).

**Recovery / mobility / supporting:**
- One mobility / movement quality specialist (Kelly Starrett-adjacent).
- One sports nutritionist with a lifting focus.

### 3.2 The outreach message

**First touch (DM, email if available, or comment → DM escalation):**

> "Hi [name], Caleb here — I run PHYZIK, a training app for lifters. I've been following your [specific content — a recent post, a lift, a coaching thread] and your approach is exactly the kind of work I'd want on our platform. We're launching publicly this month and building a small group of founding athletes. Would you be open to a short call? No ask yet — just want to show you what we're building and see if it resonates. If not, no worries at all."

Rules for the first touch:
- Reference something specific they've posted. Generic "love your content!" messages get ignored.
- Lead with curiosity, not an ask.
- Offer the call; don't pitch in DMs.
- Do not attach a pitch deck or PDF in the first message.

### 3.3 The first call

Goal: 30 minutes. Half listening, half explaining.

**Caleb's structure:**
1. Ask about their current training: how do they log? What app? What works, what doesn't?
2. Show PHYZIK live — screen share, walk through the app, show the Floor, show a session log, show the athlete roster.
3. Frame the ask: "We'd like you to train on PHYZIK for 8 weeks and tell us honestly what's missing. If you love it, we'd love to feature you. If you don't, no obligation."
4. Mention the tier they'd be in and what that includes.
5. Ask: "Any reason you wouldn't?"

What to avoid on the first call:
- Hard close. The ambassador program is a relationship, not a transaction.
- Over-promising (early-stage app will have bugs; own them).
- Comparing negatively to Hevy/Strong. Stay focused on the PHYZIK edge; let them make the comparison themselves.

### 3.4 Follow-up

- Same day: send a short email with a recap + specific next steps (early access code, Discord invite, merch shipping form).
- Day 7: check in. "How's the app feeling? Any bugs? Any wishlist items?"
- Day 14: share feedback back. "Your wishlist item on [X] — we're shipping that in week 3."
- Day 28: formally offer the ambassador tier if they've engaged. Send an explicit agreement (very lightweight — 1-page doc saying what each side offers, not a contract).

## 4. The ambassador experience

### 4.1 Onboarding (once they say yes)

- Welcome email from Caleb (personal, not templated).
- Discord/Slack invite.
- First merch shipment (Founding Athletes: hoodie + shirt + notebook; Tier B: shirt).
- Features briefing: "Here's what's shipping in the next 6 weeks, here's what we want your feedback on."
- Featured on `/community` page within 14 days of joining (Caleb owns coordinating photo/bio).

### 4.2 Ongoing rhythm

- **Monthly newsletter from Caleb** to all ambassadors. Roadmap, milestones, asks, wins. Not promotional — relational.
- **Quarterly video call** with Caleb + Sophia + all Tier A. Roadmap, feedback, open Q&A.
- **Annual in-person (when feasible).** Year 1 aim: a small dinner at a major lifting event (USAPL nationals, Hyrox global final, etc.) bringing together the core roster. Low cost, high relationship ROI.

### 4.3 The Discord/Slack

- One channel: `#ambassadors`.
- Slow-moving, not chatty. Caleb posts ~2x/week (product updates, questions, shares).
- Ambassadors post as they like — not required.
- This is the highest-trust feedback channel. Every bug report from here jumps the queue.

## 5. What each ambassador can deliver

Don't expect uniform output; these are rough ranges based on what similar programs at Strava / Whoop / Zwift saw.

| Tier | Posts/month naturally featuring PHYZIK | Referrals brought in (first 6 mo) | Feedback cycles attended |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding Athletes (6 people) | 1–3 | 10–40 each | 4–6 |
| Tier B (25 people) | 0.5–1 | 3–8 each | 1–2 |
| Tier C (open) | passive | 1–25 each | N/A |

**Honest math:** 6 Tier A × 20 avg referrals = 120 direct. 25 Tier B × 5 avg = 125 direct. Tier A content → ~30k follower reach per post × 3 posts/month × 6 ambassadors = 540k monthly impression potential. Conversion from IG impression to install is 1–3% for aligned content. So: 5–15k impressions × realistic conversion = 50–500 installs per ambassador per month.

**Realistic contribution to the 50k goal:** Ambassador program drives 3k–8k installs in first 6 weeks. That's the direct line; the indirect (content seen, reshared, discovered) is larger but unmeasurable. This is a meaningful but not *the* lever — referral + ASO + PR + paid combined will dwarf ambassadors in raw volume. **What ambassadors do is anchor trust.** Their content is what convinces the other channels' traffic to convert.

## 6. Metrics

### 6.1 What to measure

- **Ambassador roster size** (target: 6 Tier A + 15 Tier B by week 12).
- **Referral volume per ambassador** (monthly leaderboard; shared with ambassadors).
- **Content featuring PHYZIK per month, per ambassador** (manual track, weekly spot-check).
- **Retention of ambassadors** (are they still logging on PHYZIK at month 3, month 6?).
- **NPS from ambassadors** (quarterly survey: "would you recommend PHYZIK to a lifter friend, 0–10?").

### 6.2 Red flags

- Ambassador stops logging in month 2: the app is broken for their discipline. Investigate.
- Referrals stall below tier-threshold: the referral UX is broken.
- Content frequency drops to zero: we've lost them; find out why before they say it publicly.

## 7. Risks

1. **Ambassador churn.** If one of the Tier A stops engaging, it's visible (they were on the website). Mitigate: have a graceful off-ramp. Remove from site with a thank-you note, not silence.
2. **Wrong fit becomes public.** An ambassador posts something off-brand (body shaming, enhancement evangelism, gender dynamics we don't endorse). Mitigate: hard-vet at entry. Don't add fast; add carefully.
3. **Content looks like ads.** If ambassador content feels scripted, it kills trust. Mitigate: refuse to script. Ever. Trust their voice; they know their audience better than we do.
4. **Legal: disclosure.** FTC requires disclosure of material connection. If we send merch + pay, they need to mark ads/partnerships appropriately. Mitigate: clear guidelines in the onboarding doc. Ambassadors with honoraria must tag #ad or #partnership per FTC guidelines.
5. **Jealousy / status dynamics among ambassadors.** Especially if Tier A gets perks Tier B doesn't. Mitigate: transparent tier structure. Clear path Tier B → Tier A for high-performing ambassadors.

## 8. Legal — essential

- **FTC disclosure:** Ambassadors receiving anything of value (merch + cash + access) must disclose their relationship in posts per FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). At minimum: `#partner` or `#phyzikathlete` tag. Caleb: **review the FTC Endorsement Guides before first ambassador signs**. Free primer: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftc-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking.
- **Image rights:** Ambassadors should grant PHYZIK the right to use their photos/videos on our site, in our app, and in marketing. A short 1-page release doc handles this. Do not skip — using someone's likeness without written consent creates legal exposure.
- **Off-ramp:** The agreement should include a mutual 30-day termination with right to remove images from future marketing (we cannot un-print merch or un-publish IG posts, but we can stop using their likeness going forward).
- **Cross-reference `legal-compliance.md`** (WS8) for the full legal picture.
