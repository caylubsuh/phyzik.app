## PHYZIK's 3 strongest differentiators (vs. this field)

1. **Social feed where every post is a real workout.** Hevy has a feed, but it's a timeline of logs. PHYZIK's "The Floor" is designed as a social network where authenticity is enforced (no motivational reposts, every card is an actual session). **No competitor has made this the product's spine.**

2. **Free + evidence-based programming.** RP Hypertrophy charges $25-35/mo to access MEV/MAV/MRV intelligence. MacroFactor Workouts paywalls auto-progression. PHYZIK does both for free and open to everyone. That is a pricing moat only a venture-backed or founder-subsidized team can sustain — and it's our window.

3. **Squad primitive.** No competitor has shared training schedule as a group primitive. Hevy has follows; Jefit has challenges; Strava has clubs. PHYZIK's squads (shared schedule visible, missed sessions surface to the crew) is the first native social-accountability mechanic in lifting apps. This is the **compound-retention loop** of the product.

## PHYZIK's 3 biggest gaps (vs. this field)

1. **No Apple Watch app.** Strong, Hevy, MacroFactor Workouts all have Watch apps. For serious lifters, the watch isn't optional — it's where the rest timer lives. **P0 in the roadmap.** See `app-roadmap/missing-features.md`.

2. **No exercise video library.** Jefit has 1400 HD videos. Fitbod has form demos. Hevy links to YouTube. PHYZIK has 730 exercises in text. For new/unfamiliar movements this is a real activation barrier. **P1 in the roadmap.**

3. **No nutrition integration or HealthKit/sleep sync.** Lifters who care about programming *also* care about macros and recovery. MacroFactor integrates nutrition↔workouts. PHYZIK currently doesn't sync with MyFitnessPal, MacroFactor, HealthKit, or Apple Sleep. **P1 in the roadmap.** The cheap move: MyFitnessPal partner API + HealthKit read scope for sleep/weight. Don't build nutrition in-house.

## Honest takeaway for Caleb

The field breaks into three camps:
- **The loggers** (Strong, Setgraph, Liftbear) — speed-of-entry specialists
- **The AI coaches** (Fitbod, MacroFactor Workouts, RP Hypertrophy, Dr. Muscle) — paywalled programming brains
- **The social loggers** (Hevy, Jefit) — community as feature, not spine

**PHYZIK is the first to try to be a social network that happens to have a programming brain.** That's a defensible position if we execute on the feed and squads, and a confused position if we don't.

The closest actual threat is **Hevy** — mostly because they have 2M users and have spent 5 years compounding. We cannot out-logger them; we must out-social them. The second-closest is **MacroFactor Workouts** because of Strongerbyscience credibility, but their paywalled & single-player model buys us time.

**PHYZIK's weakest single claim today is "science-backed" without the visible citations.** RP Hypertrophy and MacroFactor both benefit from founders who are publishing scientists. Caleb should consider recruiting a visible science-advisor (ideally a published exercise-science PhD) to shore up this flank within 90 days. The name shouldn't have to be Mike Israetel — it could be a mid-career researcher who would take advisor equity.
